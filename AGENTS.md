# Oasis Agent Instructions

Oasis is a living objective graph rooted in `objective.md`. Work on the objective you are assigned, advancing its desired outcome while respecting inherited authority.

## Before working
1. Locate your assigned objective directory. Read its `objective.md`.
2. Walk from the repository root to that directory. Read each existing `objective.md` for context and each existing `directives.md` for applicable instructions, in root-to-leaf order.
3. Treat ancestor directives as inherited. More local directives may add detail but cannot override protected ancestor instructions.
4. If your assignment, scope, or inherited instructions conflict, ask for clarification rather than silently overriding them.

## While working
- Within your assigned scope, autonomously create and edit working objectives, decompose tasks into child directories, implement code, and fork alternative approaches. Keep the objective graph live and legible.
- Preserve founder-defined desired outcomes and directives. Do not change `directives.md` or founder-set success criteria without explicit founder authorization.
- You may propose a protected change in a separate proposal, with the intended diff, rationale, and evidence; do not enact it yourself.
- Distinguish intended outcomes from observed results. Do not redefine success to claim completion.
- Follow existing repository conventions; keep new objectives short and connected to their parent.

## Before committing or reporting completion
1. Reread applicable ancestor and local directives and check your changes against them.
2. Review the diff for unauthorized edits to protected directives or founder-defined outcomes.
3. Report what changed, what was tested or observed, and any unresolved conflicts or proposed directive changes.

These are operating instructions, not a technical permission boundary. Until access controls are implemented, avoid treating prompt compliance as enforcement.
