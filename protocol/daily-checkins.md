# Founder check-ins

These are reusable prompts, not standing authorization to spend or launch work.

## Morning — inspect
Inspect the current objective graph, active rounds, recent commits, and inherited directives. Report verified evidence, actual spending, blockers, and decisions requiring founder approval. Recommend at most three actions. Do not authorize new rounds.

## Authorize — bounded work
Propose the next round for the highest-priority problem. Include hypothesis, frozen prediction, procedure, budget, permissions, stopping condition, and required evidence. Await explicit approval before actions not already authorized.

## Evening — evaluate
Compare completed rounds with their frozen predictions. Distinguish observed evidence from agent interpretation. Recommend continue, fork, revise, terminate, or escalate; prepare but do not automatically authorize tomorrow's allocation.

## Weekly — allocate
Compare branches using progress toward parent objectives, costs, expected benefit, risk, and value of information. Propose next week's allocation and identify uncertainties. Do not treat estimates as measured outcomes or commit resources without authorization.
