# Oasis

## Objective
Build a recursively self-hosting, graph-native organization that maximizes the long-term value generated by its intelligence, operations, and capital.

**minAgentSpendmaxAgentEarn** — continuously improve the ability to turn scarce resources (tokens, compute, time, attention, and money) into valuable, verified outcomes.

## Leverage
- **Intelligence:** Increase useful reasoning per unit of compute through better models, prompts, tools, evaluation, and reusable knowledge.
- **Operations:** Improve problem decomposition, agent coordination, scientific experimentation, permissions, and execution.
- **Capital:** Allocate compute, cash, and effort toward opportunities with the best risk-adjusted expected value, including external earnings.

## Learning and R&D
Every branch should preserve evidence and learn from experiments. Invest in graph technology and general problem-solving capability when their expected future value justifies the resources. Maintain the distinction between observed returns, uncertain forecasts, and reusable research outputs.

## North Star
Long-term net value created, including realized economic returns and evidence-backed increases in future earning or problem-solving capability. Track revenue, costs, retained customers, compute efficiency, and experimental outcomes as separate measures; do not optimize a ratio at the expense of meaningful absolute progress.

## Operating principle
The objective graph is Oasis's living organization, not merely documentation about it. Every descendant must advance the root through evidence, measurable results, or a necessary capability. Prefer bounded, falsifiable experimental rounds, preserve their history, and allocate resources based on evidence, expected value, uncertainty, and risk. Do not equate an agent's self-reported success with verified value.

## Immediate priority
Ship the Graph Browser MVP and use it to run auditable agent experiments, improve the organization's own reasoning and coordination, and validate external demand. Revenue is an important test and funding source, not the sole purpose of Oasis.
