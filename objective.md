# Oasis

## Objective
Build a recursively self-hosting, graph-native organization that generates growing monthly recurring revenue.

## North Star
Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) from retained, paying customers.

## Operating principle
The objective graph is Oasis's living organization, not merely documentation about it. Every descendant must advance the root through evidence, measurable results, or a necessary capability.

## Immediate priority
Ship the Graph Browser MVP and use it to operate Oasis while validating customer demand.
