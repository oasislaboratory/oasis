# Objective

Evolve this OKR DAG into an effective system for directing Oasis and optimizing portfolios of agents and their work.

Have agents manage portfolios of objectives and experiments; measure outcomes, learn from performance, and allocate effort accordingly. Test improvements to the process itself through controlled A/B experiments and evolutionary variation, selection, and iteration.

Evaluate allocation policies against a baseline using verified objective progress, quality, cost, and elapsed time. Treat gains as hypotheses until measured; distinguish agent/work portfolios from financial portfolios, which require an explicit mandate.
